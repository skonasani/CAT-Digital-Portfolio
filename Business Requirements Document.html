01 · BUSINESS CONTEXT
CAT Digital's dealer network generates equipment telemetry, utilization, and sales data across 847 active markets spanning four US regions. Currently, regional managers receive this data through static monthly Excel reports, creating a 3 to 5 week lag between when signals appear in the data and when business decisions can be made. In a market where IIJA-driven equipment demand is accelerating and fleet utilization dips are opening a narrow aftermarket outreach window, that lag is a competitive liability. This initiative replaces static reporting with a self-serve analytics dashboard that gives regional managers and dealer principals real-time visibility into the metrics that drive CAT's two highest-priority revenue opportunities: equipment management platform adoption and aftermarket parts and service conversion.

Regional sales managers

CAT Digital product owners
Decision makers · 8 stakeholders
Need aggregate metrics on platform engagement, equipment hours logged, and aftermarket lead conversion rates to inform product roadmap prioritization.
Dealer principals
External users · ~200 dealer locations
Need their own dealership's performance benchmarked against regional peers, with fleet utilization trends and service contract expiry alerts.
Data engineering team
Technical partners · 6 engineers
Need clearly defined metric logic, data lineage documentation, and acceptable refresh latency SLAs before building the Snowflake pipeline that feeds the dashboard.

04 · METRIC DEFINITIONS
Fleet utilization rate
Engine hours used ÷ total available hours × 100. Per asset, aggregated by dealer.
Source: equipment telematics → stg.asset_hours
YoY revenue variance
(Current − prior year same period) ÷ prior year × 100. Null if prior year unavailable.
Source: dealer sales system → stg.dealer_transactions
Aftermarket lead score
Composite of utilization dip, asset age, and service contract expiry proximity. Scale 0–100.
Source: analytics.aftermarket_signals
Equipment demand index
Indexed to 100 at Jan 2023. Weighted avg of new unit orders + rental starts by category.
Source: BLS PPI + internal order data → stg.demand_signals
05 · ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
Data freshness: all KPIs reflect data no older than 24 hours. Pipeline SLA: Snowflake tables updated by 7AM CT daily.
Metric accuracy: fleet utilization rate matches source telematics within ±0.1pp for all dealer-level records in UAT dataset.
Performance: dashboard initial load ≤ 3 seconds; drill-down interactions ≤ 1.5 seconds at p95 under 50 concurrent users.
Alert delivery: utilization threshold alerts delivered via email within 15 minutes of trigger condition being met.
Access control: dealer principals may only view their own dealership's data and anonymized regional benchmarks , no cross-dealer data leakage verified in security review.
06 · RISKS AND MITIGATIONS
⚠ High: data quality
Telematics data completeness varies by dealer hardware generation. Older units report at 30-minute intervals versus real-time for newer units, creating uneven metric accuracy across the dealer base.
Mitigation: add data_completeness_pct field to all dealer records and surface a warning badge in the UI when completeness falls below 80%.
⚠ High: adoption
Regional managers have used Excel-based reports for 7 or more years. Transitioning to a self-serve dashboard requires both behavior change and targeted training, particularly for managers in regions with lower digital tool adoption.
Mitigation: co-design sessions with 3 regional manager champions before build begins; phased rollout starting with 2 pilot regions before full deployment.
◈ Medium: metric alignment
Finance and operations teams currently use different YoY revenue definitions. The inconsistency will surface immediately when the dashboard shows divergent numbers and erode stakeholder trust in the data.
Mitigation: convene a metric governance working group before the build sprint begins and document the agreed definitions in section 04 of this BRD.
◈ Medium: scope creep
Stakeholder interviews surfaced 14 features beyond the MVP scope. Without strict prioritization, the delivery timeline extends from Q3 2026 to Q1 2027 and the aftermarket outreach window may close before the platform is in dealers' hands.
Mitigation: MoSCoW prioritization enforced as documented in section 03; all "could have" features explicitly deferred to the v2 backlog.
