CAT DIGITAL · BI ANALYSIS MEMO
Infrastructure demand surge: opportunity mapping for CAT Digital's equipment analytics and aftermarket platform
Prepared by: Sivaram Konasani Date: June 2026 Audience: CAT Digital product and analytics teams Data through: Q1 2026
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
US construction spending reached $2.09T in Q1 2026, driven by IIJA disbursements shifting from planning into active project funding across highway, bridge, and utilities programs. The infrastructure segment now represents 38.6% of total spend, its highest share in a decade, while heavy equipment demand is up 7.1% YoY. Earthmoving, CAT's highest-volume product category, leads all segments at +9.2% demand growth. Separately, average fleet utilization has dipped to 72.1%, a leading indicator that idle fleet accumulation is building near-term aftermarket parts and service contract demand. This memo maps three specific actions for CAT Digital's product and analytics teams to capture both the infrastructure tailwind and the aftermarket window before they close.
FINDING 01
Infrastructure spending is accelerating , and it's concentrated geographically
IIJA disbursements shifted into active project execution in late 2025, producing a measurable step-change in infrastructure spend visible in the Census C30 data. The South and West regions collectively account for 63% of total construction activity, anchored by Texas at $198B and California at $187B. The more strategically interesting signal is the Midwest: smaller in absolute dollars at $398B but growing fastest at +6.8% YoY, as Illinois, Ohio, and Michigan highway programs ramp simultaneously. CAT Digital's current dealer analytics coverage is thinner in the Midwest relative to population-adjusted spend, making it the clearest white-space opportunity in the network.

FINDING 02
Earthmoving equipment demand is the clearest product signal
Across all equipment categories, earthmoving shows the strongest demand acceleration at +9.2% YoY, directly correlated with the infrastructure project mix driving spend: road grading, site preparation, and utility trenching all require earthmoving as the primary asset. This is CAT's highest-volume product line, which means the demand signal translates directly into dealer inventory decisions, telematics onboarding opportunities, and equipment management platform engagement. Lifting equipment follows at +7.8%, driven by vertical commercial and industrial construction. Paving and compaction lag at +6.1% and +4.4%, consistent with their role as completion-phase assets that enter the field after earthmoving project starts.

FINDING 03
Fleet utilization dip is a leading indicator for aftermarket revenue
Average fleet utilization declined 1.4 pp QoQ to 72.1% despite a strong demand environment, which initially reads as contradictory. The explanation is sequencing: IIJA project starts require equipment purchases 2 to 3 quarters before active machine hours ramp, creating a temporary inventory overhang as newly acquired assets sit in staging yards awaiting project mobilization. Historically this utilization pattern precedes a sharp aftermarket surge as those assets enter heavy use cycles and require parts, service, and extended warranty coverage. CAT Digital's aftermarket leads and insights platform is built exactly for this scenario, but the window for proactive outreach is narrow. Once utilization recovers to the 76 to 78% range, the service contract conversation shifts from proactive to reactive.

Recommendations for CAT Digital product and analytics teams
1
Expand dealer BI reporting cadence in the Midwest. Illinois, Ohio, and Michigan are simultaneously ramping IIJA highway programs and represent the highest-growth, lowest-coverage opportunity in the CAT Digital network. Adding Midwest-specific KPI views to the dealer analytics dashboard and scheduling quarterly business reviews with the top 20 Midwest dealers would capture this window before competitors establish a reporting relationship first.
2
Lead with earthmoving telematics in new dealer onboarding. With earthmoving demand up 9.2% and directly tied to the infrastructure project types dominating 2026 spend, it is the highest-value entry point for the equipment management platform. Dealers acquiring new earthmoving assets right now are the most receptive audience for telematics onboarding conversations. Onboarding materials should foreground utilization reporting and idle-time alerts as the primary value proposition.
3
Activate aftermarket outreach now, before utilization rebounds. The 72.1% fleet utilization reading is a 2 to 3 quarter leading indicator for parts and service demand. CAT Digital's aftermarket leads platform should immediately flag all assets below 65% utilization for dealer outreach, prioritizing assets acquired in the last 6 months in high-infrastructure markets. The proactive service contract positioning window closes when utilization recovers above 76%.
