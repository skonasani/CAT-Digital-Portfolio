## What's included

Three portfolio pieces built specifically for the CAT Digital BI Analyst role, each targeting a different skill area from the job description.

### [Market Intelligence Dashboard](https://skonasani.github.io/CAT-Digital-Portfolio/Dashboard.html)
Interactive BI dashboard analyzing US construction equipment market trends using Census Bureau C30 and BLS PPI data. Covers IIJA infrastructure spend acceleration, regional breakdowns, equipment demand by category, and fleet utilization KPIs. Built to mirror the reporting layer a CAT Digital analyst would produce for product owners.

Skills demonstrated: data visualization, KPI analysis, regional benchmarking, data storytelling

### [Infrastructure Demand Analysis Memo](https://skonasani.github.io/CAT-Digital-Portfolio/Memo.html)
A structured BI analyst memo translating market data into three specific product recommendations for CAT Digital's equipment analytics and aftermarket platform teams. Covers IIJA tailwinds, earthmoving demand acceleration, and fleet utilization as a leading aftermarket indicator.

Skills demonstrated: data storytelling, business recommendations, stakeholder communication, research analysis

### [Dealer Analytics Business Requirements Document](https://skonasani.github.io/CAT-Digital-Portfolio/Business%20Requirements%20Document.html)
A full BRD for a CAT Digital dealer performance analytics dashboard. Covers stakeholder needs across four user groups, six MoSCoW-prioritized functional requirements, metric definitions with data lineage, acceptance criteria, and risk analysis.

Skills demonstrated: requirements analysis, technical documentation, stakeholder management, metric governance

## Why these projects

The CAT Digital BI Analyst role lists eight core skill areas. These three documents are designed to address the ones least visible on a Finance and Business Analytics resume: requirements analysis, technical writing and documentation, business data analysis, and data storytelling.

My background in financial modeling, KPI reporting at USG, and variance analysis at Keystone covers the quantitative foundation. These projects bridge the gap to the BI-specific workflow.
