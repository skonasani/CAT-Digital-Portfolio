Key insights
Infrastructure tailwind: IIJA disbursements are accelerating into 2026
Infrastructure segment share expanded 2.3 pp YoY to 38.6%, its highest level in a decade. Earthmoving demand is up 9.2%, CAT's core product category, and the South and West regions represent 63% of total spend. The Midwest is the fastest-growing region at +6.8% YoY and is currently underweighted in dealer analytics coverage.
Fleet utilization dip signals a near-term aftermarket opportunity
Utilization declined to 72.1% despite strong demand, consistent with new fleet acquisitions staging ahead of IIJA project mobilization. This is a 2 to 3 quarter leading indicator for parts demand and service contract upsell. Proactive dealer outreach on idle assets should begin before utilization recovers above 76%.
Earthmoving is the highest-value onboarding entry point for CAT Digital
Earthmoving leads all equipment categories in demand growth at +9.2% YoY, directly tied to the road grading, site prep, and utility trenching projects driving infrastructure spend. Dealers acquiring earthmoving assets now are the most receptive audience for telematics and equipment management platform conversations.
